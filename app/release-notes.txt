Fix for the crash loop coming back.

- Fixed: the "keeps stopping" crash could still happen after a few hours of
  the app running, because Android puts a 6-hour time limit on the type of
  background service we were using. Switched to a type with no time limit.
- Added: if the app ever crashes again, more detail is now saved to the log
  (use the menu -> Submit Log after a crash to help track it down).
